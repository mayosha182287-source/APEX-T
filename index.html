01 / THE PROBLEM

Why Do Data Centers Need Better Cooling?

The rapid growth of artificial intelligence and digital services increases data-center workloads. As GPUs perform intensive computations, they generate significant heat that must be managed continuously.

💧

High Water Consumption

Conventional cooling infrastructure can consume large amounts of water, putting additional pressure on limited water resources.

⚡

High Energy Demand

Continuous cooling and heat-rejection processes increase electricity demand and operational costs.

🔥

Uneven Heat Distribution

Different GPU workloads generate different thermal loads, while conventional systems may not respond efficiently to changing temperatures.

🌍

Environmental Impact

Increasing resource consumption contributes to the environmental footprint of digital infrastructure.

03 / OUR INNOVATION

Introducing APEX-T

APEX-T proposes an intelligent cooling system that combines AI-powered thermal prediction, adaptive water-flow management, and Phase Change Material (PCM) thermal storage.

Instead of relying only on conventional reactive cooling, the system aims to predict thermal surges and manage cooling according to actual GPU requirements.

A proposed composite consisting of 85% paraffin and 15% expanded graphite acts as a thermal storage material. The PCM absorbs heat during phase change, helping manage peak thermal loads.

The system also aims to maintain a closed water-cooling loop, reducing unnecessary water loss and improving resource efficiency.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

The APEX-T Mechanism

The system follows an intelligent cycle that monitors, predicts, regulates, stores, and manages thermal energy.

01

Temperature Monitoring

Temperature sensors continuously monitor GPU and cooling-system conditions.

02

AI Prediction

An LSTM-based model analyzes time-series workload and temperature data to forecast thermal surges.

03

Adaptive Water Flow

The controller adjusts pump operation and valves according to the cooling demand.

04

PCM Thermal Storage

The paraffin-graphite composite absorbs excess thermal energy during phase change.

05

Heat Exchange

A heat exchanger helps remove stored heat and supports the PCM regeneration process.

06

Water Recirculation

The cooled water returns to the cooling loop for reuse, reducing unnecessary water loss.
